About Smart Construction Estimation System

Civil Engineering Construction Estimation Tool

Smart Construction Estimation System is a Civil Engineering project developed by Saksham Tiwari. The system helps users prepare an approximate construction cost estimate using building dimensions, foundation details, RCC information, brickwork, plaster, steel and labour inputs.

The estimator is designed to provide a structured construction cost summary for educational and preliminary estimation purposes.

Construction estimates should be verified with applicable PWD/SOR rates, site conditions, material market rates and structural design before actual construction.

Note: This is an estimated construction cost based on entered dimensions and applicable PWD/SOR rates.

Material quantities are approximate. Steel quantity must be verified through structural design.
